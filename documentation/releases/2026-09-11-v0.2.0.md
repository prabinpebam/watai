# Watai 0.2.0 production milestone

Status: candidate validated; exact promoted identities are recorded in the deployment receipt created during promotion.

## Benefits

- Safer account-isolated sync, durable sends, settings revisions, run admission, dispatch, cancellation, and streaming.
- Saved-memory policy, inventory, editing, extraction, and deletion behavior is more explicit and resilient.
- Voice capture reliably stops on mute, exit, delayed permission, and device loss.
- Search and collection workflows reject stale responses and expose recovery.
- Local export/clear controls accurately describe and enforce their scope.
- Loading failures, dialogs, keyboard interaction, contrast, and narrow-screen controls are substantially improved.

## Manual validation

1. Sign in at `https://prabinpebam.github.io/watai/` and open Settings > About; confirm version 0.2.0 and this release note.
2. Open an existing conversation, send a message, navigate away and back, then confirm the draft/reply persists.
3. Search for text in an older message and confirm the exact message becomes visible.
4. Mute/exit Voice mode during capture and confirm recording stops without sending.
5. Open Settings > Data controls and verify export/clear descriptions match the intended local-only scope.
